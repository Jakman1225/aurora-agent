AURORA AGENT EVIDENCE SDK v0.2 NON-CLAIMS

The SDK does not prove capture completeness or absence of bypass paths.
The SDK does not prove that every action performed by an agent was observed.
The SDK does not prove that a client-supplied digest corresponds to any undisclosed raw value.
DIGEST_ONLY prevents raw values from entering the SDK outbox and AURORA payload storage, but the server records a CLIENT_COMMITMENT rather than independently recomputing the source digest.
REDACTED mode does not prove that the redaction is complete or sufficient.
FULL_PAYLOAD mode may persist sensitive values locally and remotely and must be enabled deliberately.
A durable local outbox does not prove successful remote receipt until the item is ACKNOWLEDGED.
A finalized compositional graph proves consistency of stored commitments and links; it does not establish causal truth or semantic correctness of the underlying event claims.
A local commitment is not an external anchor or external timestamp.
A local VALID result does not verify an AURORA D5.4 RFC 3161 anchor export.
A declared outcome evidence strength is not physical-world truth.
An operation reference is not independent provider confirmation unless separately retrieved and verified.
JAKROW D3 outcome strength O0 remains local-runtime evidence with provider acknowledgement NOT_PRESENT.
The SDK does not establish certificate-path trust, revocation status, qualified timestamp status, legal authorization, causality, liability, admissibility, fairness, regulatory compliance, or universal runtime coverage.
The private SDK release does not establish public-release readiness, product-market fit, or standards conformance.
